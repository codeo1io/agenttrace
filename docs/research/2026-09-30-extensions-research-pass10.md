### 64 — UNPROVABLE-first evidence semantics in health and data-health reports
**Confidence: medium.** AgentMeasure's design principle — "when the evidence to decide is absent, it is disclosed, never zeroed" — is a sharper version of the fork's own truthful-posture campaign. Today agenttrace reports absent evidence as zeros in several aggregates (e.g. unclassifiable tools rank lowest and never surface: lib.rs:1653; unknown projects collapse to one bucket: insights.rs:205). Candidate: introduce a three-valued evidence taxonomy (measured / unknown / unmeasurable) at the data-health and authority surfaces so "no data" is never rendered as "0". Acceptance: a data-health report where every zero either carries provenance or is marked unmeasured; TUI renders the distinction; JSON schema gains an `evidence` field. **Why:** it is the identity the fork has been building anyway (truthful gates, provenance tokens), and the audit report shows the whole ecosystem fails this way — being the tool that *discloses* would be the differentiator, not another dashboard.
